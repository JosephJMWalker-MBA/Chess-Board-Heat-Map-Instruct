# ChessHeat Evidence-Tree Synthesis

## Purpose

Synthesize what S0/S1, T2b, T3a, and T3b have actually established before deciding whether Representation Audit deserves execution.

The synthesis distinguishes:
- EARNED
- FALSIFIED
- INCONCLUSIVE
- STILL OPEN
- FORBIDDEN INFERENCE

Negative evidence must not be turned into stronger claims than it supports.

### 1. The Governing Question

**Which squares/structures matter most to what happens next?**

This remains separated from:
- Shape $S(s|P)$
- Amplitude $A(P)$
- Human navigability (which is separate from objective Heat)

**The mathematics needs to earn the color.**

### 2. Semantic/Reproducibility Foundation

S0/S1 are enabling infrastructure, not empirical support for Heat. They established:
- sufficient chess-state identity
- branch identity
- typed evidence
- evidence-level guarantees
- projection != canonical evidence
- frozen ExperimentSpec / ExperimentResult identities
- producer/instrument identity
- deterministic manifests and artifacts

**Explicitly:** Reproducibility infrastructure increases trust in an experiment; it does not make the tested hypothesis true.

### 3. T2 Evidence

- **T2b-1 FALSIFIED**: ray transition reproduced by constituent-square event
- **T2b-2 WEAK_SUPPORT**: only against a weaker local-square baseline; context-aware square/state representation restores parity
- **T2b-3 FALSIFIED**: canonical square-native geometry matches the relation at the audited information boundary

**Earned Conclusion:** Rule-exact relational concepts remain legitimate descriptive coordinates, but the tested ray/blocker family did not earn privileged or irreducible measurement status over a sufficiently capable square/state representation. Therefore future Representation Audit must not assume that named relational concepts deserve first-class status merely because they are intelligible or decodable.

### 4. T3a Evidence

The complete result:
- **T3a-1 INCONCLUSIVE**: PV horizon
- **T3a-2 SUPPORTED**: on one mechanism-stress fixture
- **T3a-3 INCONCLUSIVE**: zero realized support

### 7. What Representation Audit Must NOT Be

Reject a future study whose main claim is merely:
"concept X can be decoded"

Because:
- decodable != causally used
- decodable != consequence-informative
- decodable != nonredundant
- decodable != useful for Heat

Also reject:
- selecting concepts because humans like their names
- using attention maps as causal evidence
- promoting relations because they visualize well
- using current T3 failures as motivation to search until something wins

### 8. Possible Representation Audit Ladder

Increasing entitlement levels:
- **R0** — decodable
- **R1** — independently reproducible decoding
- **R2** — nonredundant versus strong square/state baseline
- **R3** — consequence association on held-out evidence
- **R4** — controlled intervention sensitivity
- **R5** — incremental value to an earned spatial consequence representation

A concept reaching R0/R1 does not earn Heat inclusion.

### 9. Central Gate Before Representation Audit

**What result could Representation Audit produce that would change ChessHeat's scientific architecture?**

A future audit must have a plausible discriminating outcome. For example, a representation concept might need to demonstrate some combination of:
- independent semantic meaning
- held-out recoverability
- nonredundancy against strong canonical square/state baseline
- consequence relevance
- intervention sensitivity
- reproducibility across instruments or producers

### 10. The Unresolved Fundamental Problem

Is our next unresolved problem actually:
"What concepts are represented?"

or instead:
"What mathematical object deserves to be called spatial consequence at all?"

Based on the accumulated negative evidence, a representation audit answers the first question but cannot answer the second. Without an earned spatial consequence object (the latter), representation auditing (the former) remains disconnected from objective Heat.

### 11. Recommendation

**REPRESENTATION_AUDIT_NOT_YET_EARNED**

**Rationale:** The evidence tree establishes that we do not yet have a robust, strictly validated measurement of spatial consequence. $E_i$ and matched destination events both failed to earn status as objective consequence primitives.

R0-R2 can still answer representation questions such as decodability, reproducibility, and nonredundancy. But those results alone cannot decide Heat inclusion. R3-R5 require a scientifically earned consequence target against which association, intervention sensitivity, and incremental Heat value can be judged.

Therefore, Representation Audit must wait until the foundational consequence metric is established.
